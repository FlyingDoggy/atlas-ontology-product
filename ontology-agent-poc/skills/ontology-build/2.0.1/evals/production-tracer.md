# Production Tracer Eval

The Builder must discover exact evidence, create a typed Candidate chain, repair
deterministic closure failures, seal a review-ready Candidate Bundle, and never write
Draft, ReviewDecision, Release, or Active state.

Invariant cases:

- rates are decomposed into evidenced base numerator/denominator metrics;
- self-division and invented physical fields are rejected;
- source coverage requires exact same-target receipts;
- parseable samples are inspected without becoming normative business truth;
- a currency label cannot replace an evidenced FX conversion;
- currency-millions requires explicit scale conversion;
- ratio and percentage outputs agree with formula scale and evidenced SQL;
- a formula/SQL disagreement is repaired or recorded as a contradiction;
- all metric obligations are checked, not a convenient sample.
