# Reviewer Evaluation Contract

- Candidate, obligation, Draft, release, activation, and human-decision mutation tools
  are absent.
- Model findings remain advisory and cannot become release blockers by assertion.
- Unknown Candidate or Evidence identities are rejected.
- The exact server-supplied Candidate pages are read once each.
- High-risk Candidates are inspected through complete server-supplied batches, not
  singleton loops.
- Completion requires disclosure of the full sealed Candidate inventory.
- Reviewer context is fresh and excludes Builder scratch reasoning.
- Replayed tool-call IDs are idempotent.
- Each obligation is checked against its required Candidate collection types.
- Requirements without both Question and Analysis Pattern are found.
- Missing semantic relationships/rules/processes and missing mapping joins are found.
- Orphan collection fillers are not accepted as end-to-end closure.
- The full metric denominator is checked for unit, currency, scale, and SQL alignment.
- The run terminates within the frozen model, tool, input-token, and output-token budget.
